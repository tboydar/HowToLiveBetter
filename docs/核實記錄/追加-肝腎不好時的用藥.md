# 第 34 節第 14 條：腎功能不好的人，止痛藥和楊桃

2026-11-04。起因是兩個讀者 issue，都沒有給來源。

- issue #146：肝或腎有問題的人，要分清常用藥物的主要代謝途徑，止痛藥有的走腎、有的走肝，選在健康器官裡代謝的藥能保護受損的器官。
- issue #153：列了一串會影響 CYP3A4 的植物：石榴、歐芹、貫葉連翹、楊桃、苦橙、黑胡椒。

## 落點怎麼定的

issue #146 的原意是讓讀者自己按代謝途徑挑藥，本書不這麼寫，因為那就成了自己換藥、自己定劑量。能成立、又是「一個動作避開重後果」的，是「腎不好的人別自己買布洛芬這類藥長期吃，先問醫生」。按說明書劑量、按腎功能調藥是醫生的事，KDIGO 2024 實踐要點 4.2.1 也是寫給開藥的人的（Consider GFR when dosing medications cleared by the kidneys）。這句話放進了備註：「別自己按藥在肝裡還是在腎裡代謝去挑」。

肝那一側：對乙酰氨基酚傷肝、一日最多 2 克、別喝酒，本節第 1 條已有，不重複，只在備註裡指路。美國 NSAID 非處方藥標籤那句「先問醫生」本來就同時點了肝硬化，原樣寫進了收益欄。

issue #153 只評估了楊桃。楊桃對腎病病人的風險和藥物相互作用無關，是楊桃本身的毒素和草酸鹽，所以和「腎不好的人別自己吃止痛藥」併成一條「腎功能不好的人」條目，追加在節末。

## 四問

1. 普通讀者真會碰上嗎？中國成年人慢性腎病約 8.2%（第 16 節第 13 條，Wang 2023），高血壓病人常同時吃利尿藥和普利、沙坦類藥。楊桃在南方常見。會碰上。
2. 讀者是不是普遍不知道？慢性腎病的知曉率只有 10.0%（同上）。KDIGO 寫明 CKD G3–G5 的人每年有 18%–20% 至少用過一種可能不合適的傷腎藥，主要是 NSAIDs。楊桃傷腎在國內知道的人不多。
3. 動作是新的嗎？第 3 條講的是 NSAID 傷胃的六種高危人群，動作也是「先問醫生」，但人群和後果都不同（胃出血對腎損傷），而且本條另有「三樣一起吃」和楊桃兩個動作。兩條互相指路，沒有並：第 3 條標題只許加字，把腎病塞進去會讓標題過長，楊桃也沒地方放。
4. 有原始來源嗎？有：系統綜述兩篇（Zhang 2017、Nderitu 2013）、大型巢式病例對照（Lapi 2013）、KDIGO 2024 指南原文、美國聯邦法規原文、楊桃病例彙總三篇和病例系列兩篇。

## 關鍵原文

### NSAIDs 與腎

- Zhang 2017, BMC Nephrology 18:256, <https://doi.org/10.1186/s12882-017-0673-8>（Europe PMC 摘要）：「In the general population, the pooled odds ratio (OR) of AKI for current NSAID exposure was 1.73 (95%CI 1.44 to 2.07), with somewhat higher risk observed in older people (OR 2.51, 95%CI 1.52 to 2.68). In people with CKD, individual study OR of AKI due to current NSAID exposure ranged from 1.12 to 5.25, with pooled estimate OR 1.63 (95% CI 1.22 to 2.19).」「No study reported baseline risk of AKI in different populations meaning absolute risks could not be estimated」。老年人那個可信區間（1.52 到 2.68，點估計 2.51 靠近上限）摘要原文就是這樣，照抄。因為沒有基礎發生率，沒按「結局少見」把比值比讀成風險倍數，說人話只寫方向。慢性腎病那個合併值是 5 項研究：「of which five additionally reported NSAID risk in people with CKD」。
- Lapi 2013, BMJ 346:e8525, <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.e8525>：「A cohort of 487,372 users of antihypertensive drugs.」「During a mean follow-up of 5.9 (SD 3.4) years」「current use of a double therapy combination ... was not associated with an increased rate of acute kidney injury. In contrast, current use of a triple therapy combination was associated with an increased rate of acute kidney injury (rate ratio 1.31, 95% confidence interval 1.12 to 1.53). In secondary analyses, the highest risk was observed in the first 30 days of use (rate ratio 1.82, 1.35 to 2.46).」三樣是「diuretics with angiotensin converting enzyme inhibitors or angiotensin receptor blockers and NSAIDs」。rate ratio 按 CLAUDE.md 的 IRR 譯法寫「發病率之比」，讀法同 RR，說人話寫「多約三成」。
- Nderitu 2013, Family Practice 30(3):247-255, <https://doi.org/10.1093/fampra/cms086>：45 歲以上；「Regular-dose NSAID use did not significantly affect the risk of accelerated CKD progression; pooled odds ratio (OR) = 0.96 (95%CI: 0.86-1.07), but high-dose NSAID use significantly increased the risk ... pooled OR = 1.26 (95%CI: 1.06-1.50).」結論「The avoidance of NSAIDs in the medium term is unnecessary in patients with moderate to severe CKD, if not otherwise contraindicated.」這是反方，進了備註的「爭議」。
- KDIGO 2024（Kidney Int 105(4S):S117-S314，<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.kint.2023.10.018>；全文 PDF 取自 kdigo.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/KDIGO-2024-CKD-Guideline.pdf，pdftotext 逐字核對），第 4 章：
  - Practice Point 4.1.1：「People with CKD may be more susceptible to the nephrotoxic effects of medications.」正文寫成「可能更容易被藥物傷腎」，保留了 may。
  - Practice Point 4.1.3：「Review and limit the use of over-the-counter medicines and dietary or herbal remedies that may be harmful for people with CKD.」
  - 4.1.3 下的說明：「Indiscriminate chronic OTC NSAID use has been associated with a higher risks of kidney failure compared with nonuse and should be discouraged. However, judicious NSAID use, under careful supervision of a nephrologist, may be preferred to other pain medications such as opioids that have stronger associations with adverse events.」後半句進了備註的「爭議」。
  - 4.1.1 下的說明：「Between 18%–20% of people with CKD G3–G5 receive at least one potentially inappropriate nephrotoxic medication annually, primarily NSAIDs」。只用在本記錄的四問裡，沒進正文。
  - Figure 45（Selected herbal remedies and dietary supplements with evidence of potential nephrotoxicity）列了「Star fruit (Averrhoa carambola)」。
- 21 CFR 201.326(a)(2)(iii)(B)，eCFR 現行版（versioner API 2026-10-01 全文，要帶 --compressed）：「Ask a doctor before use if [bullet] stomach bleeding warning applies to you [bullet] you have a history of stomach problems, such as heartburn [bullet] you have high blood pressure, heart disease, liver cirrhosis, or kidney disease [bullet] you are taking a diuretic」。

### 楊桃

- Chua 2017, Clinical Toxicology 55(7):624-628, <https://doi.org/10.1080/15563650.2017.1314490>：「We reviewed totally 126 patients ... from 33 articles」，檢索 1993 到 2016 年；「The most common symptom was hiccups (65%), whereas confusion and seizure were the most common symptoms associated with mortality」；「there was no mortality in patients with normal renal function (NRF), the mortality of patients among reported cases with chronic renal insufficiency and end-stage renal disease undergoing dialysis were 36% and 27%, respectively」；「the number of SF consumed was substantially higher in the patients with NRF」。
- Yasawardene 2021, Trans R Soc Trop Med Hyg 115(9):947-955, <https://doi.org/10.1093/trstmh/trab026>：「total number of individuals=136」「Ninety-four (69.1%) patients had prior renal impairment. Renal histology showed acute oxalate nephropathy」「Oxalate and caramboxin are considered the main substances causing nephrotoxicity and neurotoxicity.」caramboxin 正文譯作「楊桃毒素」。
- Chang 2000, Am J Kidney Dis 35(2):189-193, <https://doi.org/10.1016/s0272-6386(00)70325-8>：「In the past 10 years, 20 such patients were treated in our hospital when they developed clinical symptoms after eating the fruit or drinking star fruit juice.」「Eight patients died, including one patient with a serum creatinine level of 6.4 mg/dL who had not yet begun dialysis.」「Death occurred within 5 days」。摘要沒寫醫院所在地，正文只寫「一家醫院」。
- Neto 2003, Nephrol Dial Transplant 18(1):120-125, <https://doi.org/10.1093/ndt/18.1.120>：32 名尿毒症病人；「Patients who were promptly treated with haemodialysis, including those with severe intoxication, recovered without sequelae. Patients with severe intoxication who were not treated or treated with peritoneal dialysis did not survive.」作者單位在巴西（Ribeirão Preto），正文寫「巴西一組」。
- Aranguren 2017 系統綜述（Saudi J Kidney Dis Transpl 28(4):709-715，PMID 28748871，Crossref 查不到 DOI）：123 人、8 個國家、28 人（22%）死亡。和 Chua 2017 的病例大量重疊，數字取 Chua 那篇（按腎功能分了死亡率），這篇沒進來源欄。

## 沒用的來源

- 上海市衛健委網站 2021-09-02「顏值高、口感好，但這個水果卻可能變身『腎臟殺手』」（wsjkw.sh.gov.cn/spwsyyy/20210902/c531581c5c1141cc99b7da451e17cc12.html）：頁尾寫明「摘自上海交通大學醫學院附屬瑞金醫院微信公眾號」，按引用規則不收。文中把楊桃傷腎的機制寫成「腎小球毛細血管基底膜出現損傷……變態反應」，和上面綜述的「草酸鹽腎病、楊桃毒素」不是一回事，也是不收的理由之一。它建議高血壓、糖尿病病人也儘量別吃、嬰幼兒不適合吃，這兩點沒找到原始文獻，沒寫。
- 美國腎臟基金會（NKF）患者頁「Always AVOID star fruit」：行業協會的患者教育材料，有 KDIGO 指南就夠了，沒引。
- KDIGO 的 sick day rules（生病脫水時暫停 NSAIDs 等藥）：指南自己寫明「there is a paucity of evidence」，還提到病人搞錯時可能有害，沒寫。

## issue #153 其餘植物不收的理由

- 苦橙：第 13 條（西柚）已寫酸橙（「常用來做橙子醬」）同樣有呋喃香豆素作用。
- 貫葉連翹：第 16 節第 4 條（別為了試偏方、保健品停掉正規治療）的收益欄和備註已寫它會減弱抗排異藥、抗凝藥等（NCCIH）。
- 石榴：兩項健康志願者試驗都沒看出對 CYP3A 探針藥咪達唑侖有影響。Farkas 2007（J Clin Pharmacol, doi:10.1177/0091270006298359）：「Pomegranate juice does not impair clearance of oral or intravenous midazolam」，同一試驗裡西柚汁有影響；Misaka 2011（Clin Ther, doi:10.1016/j.clinthera.2011.02.012）連喝 2 週，AUC 幾何均數比 1.02（0.95–1.09）。體外有抑制、人體沒看到。
- 黑胡椒：能找到的是胡椒鹼提純物的小試驗，譬如 Bedada 2017 每天 20 毫克胡椒鹼連吃 10 天，卡馬西平 AUC 升 47.9%（12 人）。那是提純物劑量，不是吃飯撒的胡椒，沒找到日常食用量有臨床意義的證據。
- 歐芹：沒找到人體藥代或臨床病例證據。

## 統計口徑

本條成本標籤：錢=0 時間=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口徑=死亡率，不加人群標籤（慢性病沒有對應值）。收益定「中」憑判斷：急性腎損傷的數字是比值比和發病率之比，沒有死亡降幅；楊桃的死亡比例來自病例報告，有發表偏倚，算不出照做能少幾成。證據定 B，標「爭議」（常規劑量是否傷腎，Nderitu 2013 與 KDIGO 後半句）。
